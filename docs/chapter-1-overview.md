# HueyBot Overview

HueyBot is an intelligent personal assistant designed to help users effectively manage their mental health and well-being. With impressive statistics showing a growing user base, HueyBot addresses the need for accessible and real-time mental health support.

## Statistics
- **Active Users:** 10,000+
- **Sessions Conducted:** 50,000+
- **User Satisfaction Rate:** 95%

## What it is
HueyBot uses advanced algorithms to provide psychological analysis and cater to individual user needs by offering personalized advice.
