# Key Features of HueyBot

## Psychological Analysis
HueyBot offers in-depth psychological analysis to help users understand their behaviors and tendencies in the context of trading and investments.

## Real-Time Advice
Receive real-time advice based on market conditions and personal investment profiles, ensuring users make informed decisions.

## Portfolio Management
HueyBot provides intuitive tools for managing investment portfolios, including performance tracking and asset allocation.

## Goal Setting
Set personalized financial goals and track progress with actionable insights and reminders.

## Learning Resources
A library of learning resources, including articles, videos, and courses, to enhance user knowledge about trading and investments.

## Risk Alerts
Real-time risk alerts that notify users of potential risks or significant market changes, empowering them to mitigate losses.
